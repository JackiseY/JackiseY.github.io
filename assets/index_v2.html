01 / Background

About Me

I am Jingbo (Jackie) Yue, a Computer Engineering student at Purdue University and an undergraduate researcher at the Video and Image Processing Laboratory (VIPER), advised by Prof. Fengqing Zhu.

My research spans multimodal large language models (MLLMs), computer vision, visual grounding, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Recent work investigates evidence-driven food-item verification, segmentation-assisted recovery, and ingredient-level grounding for reliable image-based dietary assessment.

I previously conducted computer vision research at Purdue's Agricultural & Biological Engineering department, studying image-based plant phenotyping and herbicide-injury assessment. I am also interested in the intersection of learning-based perception, classical image processing, and embedded systems.
