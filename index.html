Computer Engineering Student & Undergraduate Researcher

My research focuses on computer vision and multimodal learning, with a particular interest in recognizing individual objects and estimating their physical properties from images through structured, chain-of-thought reasoning. My current work applies these ideas to dietary assessment: identifying the foods in a meal, estimating their portions, and connecting them to nutritional reference data. I develop methods that verify food identities and image regions, recover overlooked items, and address errors that propagate from recognition to nutrition estimation. I am also exploring learning-based approaches that use food appearance, shape, and spatial relationships to improve portion and nutrient estimation across diverse cuisines from the United States, Europe, Oceania, and Asia.

01 / Background

About Me

I am Jingbo (Jackie) Yue, a Computer Engineering student at Purdue University. I conduct undergraduate research in the Video and Image Processing Laboratory (VIPER), advised by Prof. Fengqing Zhu.

My recent research examines the reliability of image-based dietary assessment: how food regions can be verified using semantic and geometric evidence, how missed items can be recovered through targeted segmentation review, and how identified foods can be grounded to nutritional reference data.

I previously conducted computer vision research on image-based plant phenotyping and herbicide-injury assessment at Purdue's ABE Plant Sensor Lab (LeafSpec), led by Prof. Jian Jin. Beyond these applications, I enjoy connecting algorithmic image analysis with practical systems engineering.

02 / Research

Research Interests

Research questions and methods
01 · Visual perception

Computer Vision & Geometric Reasoning

How can segmentation and spatial consistency help distinguish visible food items from false detections and recover missed items? This direction studies region-level evidence and object-centric recognition.

02 · Reliable inference

Multimodal Verification & Recovery

How can multimodal models check each proposed food item against the image? Our food-item verification work uses region checks and a targeted review of unresolved visual evidence, followed by independent re-verification of recovered items.

03 · Grounded estimation

Food Recognition & Portion Estimation

How do food identification, portion estimates, and reference-data selection affect the final nutrition estimate? Our Open-KNEAD work studies item-level retrieval from FNDDS and auditable portion estimation.
