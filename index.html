01 · Background

About Me

I am Jingbo (Jackie) Yue, a Computer Engineering student at Purdue University and an undergraduate researcher in the VIPER Lab, advised by Professor Fengqing Zhu.

My research interests include multimodal large language models (MLLMs), computer vision, and agentic AI workflows. My recent work explores food-item verification, visual grounding, and recovery mechanisms for image-based nutrition estimation.
